Share once. Everyone benefits.

The whole point of Trexpanda: the great snippets your teammates write become yours too — automatically.

1

One person sets up the library

A teammate points Trexpanda at a shared folder (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive) or a link, and publishes the starter snippets.

2

Invite the team

Send them the installer and the same library location. They paste it into Settings once — that's the whole setup.

3

Everyone stays in sync

When anyone adds or improves a snippet, it flows to the shared library and every teammate picks it up on their next sync.

You write → shared library → your whole team types it  ·  and their snippets flow back to you the same way.
