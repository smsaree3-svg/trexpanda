FAQ

Good questions

What is a text expander?

It types your long, repeated text for you. You set up a short shortcut once, like ;addr for your address, and whenever you type it, Trexpanda replaces it with the full text. You can read the full walkthrough on the help page.

Which devices does it work on?

Windows and macOS on the computer, and Android on the phone through the Trexpanda keyboard. On Android you can also turn on any-keyboard mode, so shortcuts expand inside Gboard or any other keyboard. An iPhone version is on the way.

What happens after the free trial?

You get 30 days with everything, no card needed. After that, Pro keeps it all going for $3 a month or $30 a year. Your snippets are never deleted, and you can subscribe whenever you are ready.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Billing is handled by Stripe and you can cancel whenever you like. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for.

Is my data private?

Your snippets are tied to your own account and are only there to sync between your devices. Full details are on the privacy page.
