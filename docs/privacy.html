Trexpanda Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Trexpanda is a product of Lumisha LLC.

Trexpanda is a text-expander. On Android it includes an on-screen keyboard (an input method), and on iPhone a keyboard extension, that inserts snippets you create when you type their short triggers. This policy explains what Trexpanda does and does not collect, and how your information is handled.

What the keyboard does NOT do

The Trexpanda keyboard does not log, store, or transmit the text you type in other apps. It watches your typing only to detect your own snippet triggers so it can replace them, and it does this on your device. It does not record passwords, messages, search queries, or any other content you type. Trexpanda does not sell your data, and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

What we store, and why

Your snippets. The triggers and replacement text you create are the core of the product. They are stored on your device so the keyboard works offline.

Cloud sync (optional). If you sign in, your snippets are synced to your account so they are available across your devices (for example your computer and your phone). This sync is provided through a secure cloud database provider, and access is restricted to your own account.

Account information. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive a basic account identifier and your email address to create and identify your account. We do not receive your Google password. If you create an account with an email address and password, we store your email address; your password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider.

Friends and sharing (optional). If you use friends and shared libraries, your chosen username and display name are visible to people you connect with, and libraries you choose to share are visible to those friends.

Payments. Subscriptions are processed by our payment processor. We receive the status of your subscription, not your full card details.

How your information is used

Your snippets and account information are used only to provide the product: to expand your snippets, to keep them in sync across your devices, and to manage your account and subscription. We do not use your content for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

Your snippets are kept until you delete them or close your account. Deleting a snippet removes it from your devices and your synced account.

Deleting your account. In the Trexpanda app, sign in and choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all its cloud data (synced snippets, shared libraries, friends and profile) right away. If you have an active paid subscription, cancel it first so you are not charged again. You can also request deletion without the app: see trexpanda.com/delete-account. Snippets stored on your own devices stay there until you remove the app.

Security

Cloud data is protected by access controls that restrict every record to its owner, and connections to the cloud use encryption in transit (HTTPS).

Children

Trexpanda is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
