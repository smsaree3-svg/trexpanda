Getting started

How Trexpanda works

Trexpanda types your long, repetitive text for you. You set up a short shortcut once, type it in any app, and the full text appears instantly. Here is everything you need to get going on your computer and your phone.

The one idea behind it

Everything in Trexpanda is a snippet. A snippet is just two things: a short shortcut you type, and the full text it turns into.

;addr → 221B Baker Street, London

You type the shortcut ;addr anywhere you can type (an email, a chat, a form, a note). Trexpanda sees it and instantly replaces it with the full address. You save the typing, every single time. Shortcuts are not case sensitive, so ;addr, ;ADDR and ;Addr all do the same thing.

💻Set up on Windows or Mac

1

Download and open Trexpanda

Get the app from the Download page and open it. It runs quietly in the background and sits in your menu bar (Mac) or system tray (Windows), ready whenever you type.

2

Sign in

Sign in with your Google account. This is what keeps your snippets backed up and in sync, so the ones you make on your computer also show up on your phone, and the other way around.

3

Create your first snippet

Click New snippet. In Shortcut type something short and unusual like ;sig. In Expands to type the full text you want, for example your email signature. Save it.

Tip: start every shortcut with a symbol such as ; or /. That way it never fires by accident in the middle of a normal word.
4

Use it anywhere

Switch to any app, start typing, and when you type your shortcut it turns into the full text on the spot. As you type, a small suggestion popup shows matching shortcuts, so you can pick one even if you forget the exact name.

5

Turn it on or off, and import in bulk

In Settings you can switch expansion off for a moment if you need to, and switch it back on. You can also import a whole list of snippets at once from a CSV file, or export yours to a CSV to keep a copy.

📱Set up on your phone

1

Install the app

Install Trexpanda on your phone from the Download page. Trexpanda works as its own keyboard, so instead of changing every app, you type on the Trexpanda keyboard and your shortcuts work everywhere.

2

Turn on the Trexpanda keyboard

Open the app and follow the one-time setup. It takes you to your phone's keyboard settings where you switch Trexpanda on, then allow it. This is a normal step your phone asks for any new keyboard.

3

Switch to it when you type

In any app, tap a text box, then tap the small globe or keyboard icon to switch to the Trexpanda keyboard. Your shortcuts only expand while you are typing on the Trexpanda keyboard.

4

Sign in to sync

Sign in with the same Google account you used on your computer. Every snippet you made there now appears on your phone automatically, and new ones you add on the phone flow back to your computer.

5

Type a shortcut

Type your shortcut, for example ;sig, and it expands right in the text box. Matching shortcuts also show in the strip above the keys, so you can tap one instead of typing the whole thing.

The app also shows how much time you have saved, based on the number of words Trexpanda has typed for you.

Smart pieces you can drop into any snippet

Inside the text a snippet expands to, you can use these special pieces. Trexpanda fills them in fresh every time.

Write thisYou get
{date}Today's date, like 2026-10-07 (updates every time you use it)
{time}The current time, like 14:09
{datetime}Date and time together
$|Where the cursor should land after the text appears. Handy for a greeting like Dear $|, so you can keep typing the name right away.

If something is not working

A quick checklist for the most common questions.

I typed my shortcut but nothing happened

Run through these in order. First, make sure expansion is switched on in Settings. On the phone, make sure you are actually typing on the Trexpanda keyboard (tap the globe or keyboard icon to switch to it). Then check that you typed the shortcut exactly as you saved it, for example ;sig, and remember a shortcut only fires once the whole thing is typed.

My snippets are not showing up on my other device

Sync works through your account. Sign in with the same Google account on both your computer and your phone. Once both are signed in to the same account, your snippets appear on both, usually within a few seconds. A working internet connection is needed for a fresh sync.

Does the case of the shortcut matter?

No. Shortcuts are not case sensitive, so ;brb, ;BRB and ;Brb all expand the same snippet. This works the same on your computer and on your phone.

A shortcut keeps firing when I did not mean it to

That usually means the shortcut is a normal word, so it triggers while you write. Give it a symbol at the start, like ; or /, for example ;addr instead of addr. Short symbol shortcuts almost never fire by accident.

Can I move my snippets in and out?

Yes. On both the computer and the phone you can import a list of snippets from a CSV file and export your snippets to a CSV. This is the easy way to bring in a big batch at once or keep your own backup.

Is my data private?

Your snippets are tied to your own account and are only there to sync between your devices. You can read the full details on the privacy page.
