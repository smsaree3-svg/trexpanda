The text you type to fire the expansion. Prefix with a symbol like ; or / to avoid firing on normal words.

Formatting (bold, italic, lists, links, images) is kept when the macro pastes into rich apps like email & docs. Tokens: {date} {time} {datetime}.

An image pastes inline when the trigger fires. A file is copied to your clipboard so you can paste it as an attachment (or its path). Kept small snippets sync best — large files bloat the team library.
