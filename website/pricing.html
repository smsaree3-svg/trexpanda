Questions

What happens when the 30 days end?

If you haven't subscribed, the app goes read-only — your snippets stop expanding and you can't add new ones. Nothing is deleted; subscribe (or redeem a code) and everything switches straight back on.

How do I upgrade?

Open Trexpanda, go to the Account tab, and click “Upgrade to Pro.” Checkout opens securely in your browser; your plan switches to Pro automatically when you're done. If you have a coupon code, redeem it in the same place.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription from the Account tab (Manage subscription), which opens the secure billing portal. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Is my typing data private?

Trexpanda only acts on the short triggers you define. Your snippet library syncs to your own account; it isn't shared or sold. See our Privacy Policy for the full detail.
